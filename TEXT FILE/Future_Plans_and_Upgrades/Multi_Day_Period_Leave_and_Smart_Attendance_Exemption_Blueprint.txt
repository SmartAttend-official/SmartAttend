1. THE PROBLEM & THE SOLUTION (Executive Summary)
-------------------------------------------------
BEFORE THIS FEATURE:
1. Single-Day Limitation: Students had to submit multiple individual leave
   requests for a continuous sick leave or multi-day event.
2. The "False Absenteeism Panic" Loop: When a student had an officially approved
   leave, the attendance system still marked them as "Absent" during class
   attendance. The system then automatically sent alarming "Absenteeism Alert"
   emails to their parents, causing unnecessary distress and complaints.
3. Attendance Penalty: Leave days were either counted as absent (reducing the
   student's attendance percentage) or excluded, leading to discrepancies.

AFTER THIS FEATURE (SmartAttend Multi-Day & Exemption Engine):
1. Continuous Period Leave: Students apply once for a date range (e.g., Nov 2
   to Nov 7), select a category (Medical, Academic, Emergency, Other), and can
   use quick-duration chips (+2d, +3d, +5d, +7d). Total days auto-calculate.
2. Instant Parent Visibility: The moment a request is submitted, parents
   receive an official email notification detailing the period and reason.
3. Advisory Visual Cues for Faculty: On both Classroom Face Scan and Lab
   Attendance screens, students on active leave are highlighted in amber with
   an "On Leave: X Days" badge, and their camera detection box turns amber.
4. Zero False Absent Alerts: When attendance is saved, any student on active
   leave (Approved or Pending) is automatically filtered out from absent emails.
5. Fair Attendance Credit (The Credit Model): Students on approved leave
   are credited identically to Present students. Both their Overall Total
   (Classes_Attended / Total_Classes) AND their Subject-Wise Attendance
   (SubjectAttendance) increase, preserving their percentage safely.


2. END-TO-END WORKFLOW (From Submission to Credit)
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Student Submission (Student Portal / Mobile APK)
  • Student selects "Multiple Days" mode on their dashboard.
  • Chooses Start Date and End Date (or taps a duration chip).
  • Form calculates duration (inclusive calendar days) and enforces End >= Start.
  • Overlap Guard: Backend rejects the request if dates conflict with any existing
    Pending or Approved leave for that student.
  • On submit: An instant email is dispatched to the registered Parent email.

Step 2: Faculty Review & Notification
  • Professor visits the Leave Requests review panel.
  • Pending requests display student details, category, date span, and reason.
  • Upon Approval or Rejection: An automated email with the decision and date
    summary is dispatched to both the student and their parent.

Step 3: Taking Attendance (Classroom Camera & Lab Panels)
  • When a professor opens Classroom Face Scan or Lab Attendance, the system
    synchronizes the session date (via an interactive date picker badge).
  • The system fetches all active leaves covering that date.
  • If a student is on leave:
      - Their table row glows in amber with an "On Leave (Xd)" badge.
      - In the camera view, their detected face is outlined in amber.
      - Their manual status toggle pre-selects the "Leave" button.
      - Professor can manually override to Present or Absent if needed.

Step 4: Session Saving & Attendance Calculation
  • When the professor clicks "Save Session":
      a. Individual records are written to AttendanceHistory with Status: 'Leave'.
      b. Overall totals: Classes_Attended and Total_Classes both increment in
         the students table. Attendance percentage remains unpenalized.
      c. Subject breakdown: In ?sheet=SubjectAttendance, the student's attended
         count for that specific subject also increments by 1.
      d. False Alert Suppression: The automated email dispatcher checks the
         exempted active leave list and strips out all leave students.
         Parents never receive an absent warning for permitted leave days.


3. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE (LeaveRequests Table)
----------------------------------------------
The system stores multi-day leaves in Supabase PostgreSQL:

  Column         Type          Description
  -------------  ------------  ----------------------------------------------
  startDate      DATE          First calendar day of the leave (YYYY-MM-DD)
  endDate        DATE          Last calendar day of the leave  (YYYY-MM-DD)
  durationDays   INTEGER       Total inclusive calendar days (default: 1)
  leaveType      VARCHAR(50)   Category: Medical / Academic / Emergency / Other
  status         VARCHAR(20)   Pending / Approved / Rejected
  reason         TEXT          Student's stated reason for absence
  studentId      VARCHAR(50)   Student roll number / identifier
  date           DATE          Legacy column preserved for backwards compatibility

Active Leaves Query:
  A student is considered on active leave for a session date if:
    status IN ('Approved', 'Pending') AND
    startDate <= sessionDate AND
    endDate >= sessionDate


4. THE ATTENDANCE CREDIT ENGINE (Code Implementation)
-----------------------------------------------------
SmartAttend uses the "Credit Model" rather than the "Exemption Model":

1. Overall Attendance (server/server.js `save_session`):
   When calculating the student's overall attendance ledger:
     if (['present', 'late', 'leave'].includes(status)) {
       uniqueAttended.add(sessionKey);
     }
     uniqueSessions.add(sessionKey);
   Result: Classes_Attended + 1, Total_Classes + 1. Percentage is protected.

2. Subject-Wise Attendance (server/server.js `SubjectAttendance`):
   When querying subject statistics for the student dashboard breakdown:
     if (['present', 'late', 'leave', 'on leave', 'excused'].includes(hStatus)) {
       subjectStats[hSub].attendedDates.add(hDate);
     }
   Result: Subject Attended count increments alongside Total sessions.
   The subject card perfectly mirrors the overall attendance ledger.

3. Logs and Reports:
   • History Table (html/History.html & backend/history.js): Displays an amber
     `.badge-warning` tag for leave records instead of red danger.
   • Session Email Reports: Faculty reports show 'Leave' in amber (#f59e0b).
   • Analytics Reports: Credit leave students as attended in class metrics.


5. EMAIL NOTIFICATION ECOSYSTEM
--------------------------------
The feature maintains four automated HTML email triggers via Google Apps Script:
  1. Leave Submission (To Parent):
     Notifies parents that their child applied for leave with dates and reason.
  2. Leave Approval (To Student & Parent):
     Confirms professor approval with full date range and total days.
  3. Leave Rejection (To Student & Parent):
     Informs them of rejection with professor remarks.
  4. Absent Alert Suppression (To Parent & Student):
     Intercepts absent alert emails during save_session or manual dispatch,
     ensuring leave students are omitted from absent notifications.


6. BUILT-IN SAFEGUARDS & EDGE CASES
-----------------------------------
1. Overlapping Leave Prevention:
   A student cannot submit overlapping leave windows. If an existing Pending
   or Approved leave overlaps any requested date, the submission is blocked.
2. Inclusive Calendar Math:
   Duration is calculated as: Math.round((end - start) / (1000*60*60*24)) + 1.
   A leave from Friday to Monday correctly calculates as 4 days.
3. Pending Leave Exemption:
   Students whose leave is still pending review are protected from false absent
   alerts. They are not penalized while faculty reviews the application.
4. Faculty Override Authority:
   Visual highlighting is advisory. Faculty can manually change the toggle to
   'Present' or 'Absent' before saving.
5. Early Return / Physical Presence:
   If a student returns early from leave and their face is scanned by the camera,
   face detection recognizes them and automatically switches them to Present.


7. FUTURE ENHANCEMENTS
----------------------
