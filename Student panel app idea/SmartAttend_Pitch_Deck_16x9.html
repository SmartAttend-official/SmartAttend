Problem Statement 02 / 10

The Hidden Cost of Traditional Campus Attendance

Paper registers, basic apps, and biometric hardware cause friction across 55,000+ Indian institutions.

Massive Classroom Time Bleed

Professors lose 10 to 15 minutes of every 60-minute lecture manually calling roll numbers across 60–120 students, wasting ~15% of annual instructional time.

Rampant Proxy & Buddy Punching

Manual callouts and static QR codes make proxy attendance effortless. Institutional attendance logs become unreliable and unverified.

The "75% Blind Spot"

Students only discover they are below the mandatory 75% attendance threshold weeks before finals—when it is mathematically impossible to recover.

Target Impact: Eliminating administrative friction & proxy manipulation across Indian higher education SmartAttend AI
The Solution 03 / 10

SmartAttend AI: Frictionless Verification & Student Transparency

A multi-modal intelligence platform built specifically for real campus lecture and lab environments.

Lecture Halls: Contactless Face Recognition

Rapid computer vision scans student faces via professor webcams. Converts facial descriptors into secure 128-d vector embeddings without expensive biometric hardware.

  • Sub-second multi-face verification
  • Zero dedicated biometric machine maintenance

Labs & Practicals: Anti-Proxy PIN + Device Lock

Dynamic 6-digit rolling PIN combined with hardware-level device fingerprinting ensures students can only submit attendance from their own physical smartphone.

  • Prevents multi-account proxy submissions
  • Real-time student recovery formula built into the mobile app
Dual verification eliminates proxy submissions while protecting student time SmartAttend AI
